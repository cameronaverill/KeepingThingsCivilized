- **Topic:** Cities should limit their local police's cooperation with federal immigration enforcement.
- **Kind of claim:** legal or other non-numerical

- **Planted false claim:** In Illinois, there's a state law about sanctuary policies, but it generally still allows local law enforcement to hold people where there's a civil detainer request

**Participant A (message 3)**

Drawing that bright line of noncooperation treats every contact with police as the same, when it isn't.

**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim in message 4 about Illinois's sanctuary law appears to have the effect reversed: Illinois's TRUST Act generally prohibits local law enforcement from holding people on civil immigration detainer requests, rather than generally allowing it. This can be checked against the text of the Illinois TRUST Act.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Multiple independent sources, including the Illinois Attorney General's office and local news coverage, describe the Illinois TRUST Act as generally prohibiting local law enforcement from honoring civil immigration detainer requests or otherwise assisting federal civil immigration enforcement, rather than generally allowing such holds. This is the opposite of what the claim states about the law's effect.
