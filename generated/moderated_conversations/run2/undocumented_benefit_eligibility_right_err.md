**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim in message 4 that unlawful immigrants can typically get food stamps and social security overstates eligibility. Undocumented immigrants are generally ineligible for federal SNAP benefits and for Social Security benefits in their own right, though US citizen children in such households may sometimes qualify for benefits based on their own status.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources indicate undocumented immigrants are generally ineligible for federal SNAP benefits and for Social Security benefits in their own name; eligibility for most federal means-tested programs is restricted to citizens and certain qualified noncitizens. US-citizen children of undocumented parents can sometimes receive SNAP or other benefits based on the child's own eligible status, with the household's benefit calculated accordingly, but this is different from the parents themselves receiving aid. The claim that unlawful immigrants can 'typically' get these benefits, including for their kids, overstates what current federal rules allow.
