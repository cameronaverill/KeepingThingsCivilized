**AI moderator** (asked for a source)

Could a source be given for the claim in message 4 about Illinois law restricting stops, searches, or holds based on immigration status or civil detainer requests?

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

This matches the Illinois TRUST Act (5 ILCS 805), which bars law enforcement agencies statewide from stopping, arresting, searching, or detaining a person solely based on immigration status, and separately bars holding someone solely on the basis of a civil immigration detainer or warrant. Statutory text and summaries from the Illinois Attorney General's office and advocacy groups consistently describe this as a statewide restriction, not a local-option policy.

Sources:
- GENERAL PROVISIONS (5 ILCS 805/) Illinois TRUST Act. (https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=3818&ChapterID=2)
- Guidance Summary: Key Provisions of the Illinois TRUST Act June 2025 (https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/Page-Attachments/GuidanceSummaryofTrustAct.pdf)
- Illinois ICE guidance: What we know about raids and local law enforcement's response (https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/illinois-ice-guidance-law-enforcement-response)
