Protecting routine interactions, like reporting a crime or seeking medical help, from immigration consequences is narrowly about preserving trust, not about shielding anyone from real accountability for an actual offense, in my view.

I believe public safety is better served when there's a clear, uniform expectation that the law applies equally, without carve-outs based on status. This is the claim I am relying on.
