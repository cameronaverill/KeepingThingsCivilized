Could a source be given for the claim in message 4 about Connecticut's rules on civil immigration holds and the exceptions for terror watch list status or severe violent felony convictions?

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources confirm the substance of this: Connecticut's TRUST Act bars state and local law enforcement from detaining someone on a civil ICE detainer alone unless a judicial warrant is also presented, the person is on a terrorist watch list, or the person has been convicted of a class A or B felony (serious violent crimes such as murder, sexual assault, or kidnapping). The claim's framing as terror watch list or severe felony conviction being the only exceptions omits that a judicial warrant is also an independent basis for compliance, and the felony category is specifically class A or B, not a broader 'severe violent felony' standard.
