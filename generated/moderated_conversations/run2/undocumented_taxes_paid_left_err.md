**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim that unlawful immigrants pay trillions in taxes per year appears far above published estimates. Commonly cited figures for taxes paid by undocumented immigrants in the United States are in the tens of billions of dollars annually, not trillions.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Published estimates, including a widely cited 2024 analysis from the Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy, put annual tax contributions from undocumented immigrants at roughly 97 billion dollars in federal, state and local taxes, not trillions. Independent sources consistently describe this figure in the tens of billions, so a claim of trillions per year is well above what the available estimates support.
