**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The figure that unauthorized immigrants are incarcerated at rates 90-95% lower than native-born citizens appears to overstate the disparity found in most published studies, which generally report a lower but smaller gap. It may be worth checking the original source for this figure to confirm the exact percentage.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Cato Institute analyses of Texas and national incarceration data find illegal immigrants incarcerated at roughly half the rate of native-born U.S. citizens (about 50% lower), and legal immigrants at an even lower rate (around 70-75% lower); multiple other studies, including NIJ-funded research on Texas arrest data, similarly describe gaps in the range of 50% to 75% rather than 90-95%. Independent sources consistently confirm immigrants, including unauthorized immigrants, have lower incarceration rates than native-born citizens, but the specific 90-95% figure appears considerably larger than the gap reported in the studies reviewed.
