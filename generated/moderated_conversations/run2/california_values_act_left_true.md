**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

The description broadly matches California's SB 54 (California Values Act), which generally bars state and local agencies from using personnel or resources to investigate, detain, or arrest people for federal civil immigration enforcement, while allowing discretionary cooperation in defined circumstances. The relevant exceptions are somewhat narrower and more technical than a simple 'violent or serious felony' label: cooperation is permitted mainly for people with specified current charges or prior convictions on an enumerated list of serious or violent felonies (and some related wobbler offenses), not for felony convictions in general. So the core framing is accurate, but the carve-out is more precisely defined by statute than the summary suggests.
