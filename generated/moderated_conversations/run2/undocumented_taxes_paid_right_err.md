**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim in message 4 that unlawful immigrants don't pay taxes is not accurate as a general statement. Undocumented immigrants commonly pay sales and property taxes, and many also pay payroll or income taxes using Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers; this is documented by sources such as the Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources, including IRS data and a widely cited Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy study, indicate undocumented immigrants paid nearly $100 billion in federal, state, and local taxes in 2022, including sales, property, payroll, and income taxes, with many using ITINs to file federal returns. This contradicts the blanket statement that unlawful immigrants do not pay taxes.
