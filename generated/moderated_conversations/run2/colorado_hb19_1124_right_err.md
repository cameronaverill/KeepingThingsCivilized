**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Colorado's 2019 law (HB19-1124) restricts federal immigration authorities' access to nonpublic jail areas, but independent sources and the bill text itself indicate this restriction does not apply when federal agents present a warrant issued by a federal judge or magistrate; in that case access is permitted. This is consistent with the pattern described in the discussion, where such laws typically bar access absent a judicial warrant rather than barring it even when one exists, so the claim as stated appears to reverse how the law actually operates.
