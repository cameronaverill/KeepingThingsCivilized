The figure in message 1626 of a 90% to 95% lower incarceration rate for unauthorized immigrants appears to overstate published research. Studies generally find unauthorized immigrants are incarcerated at lower rates than native-born citizens, but by a smaller margin than stated here; this is worth checking against the original source before relying on it further.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Leading estimates, including Cato Institute analyses of Census and Texas criminal-justice data, consistently find unauthorized immigrants are incarcerated at roughly 40-55% lower rates than native-born U.S. citizens (for example, about 674 vs. 1,195 per 100,000 in the most recent year examined), not 90-95% lower as stated. The direction of the claim is supported by independent research, but the magnitude cited substantially overstates the gap found in published studies.
