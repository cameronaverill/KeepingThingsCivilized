**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

This matches the general structure of California's SB 54 (California Values Act), which restricts state and local law enforcement from using resources to investigate, detain, or arrest people for civil immigration enforcement purposes, with exceptions allowing cooperation when a person has been convicted of certain serious or violent felonies (as listed in state statute) within a specified look-back period. Independent sources, including legal advisories and summaries of the statute, support this general description, though the exact list of qualifying offenses and time limits is more detailed than stated.
