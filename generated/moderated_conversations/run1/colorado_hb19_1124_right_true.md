**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources confirm both parts of this claim. Colorado statute (C.R.S. 24-76.6-102, enacted via HB19-1124) bars state and local law enforcement from arresting or detaining someone based solely on a civil immigration detainer, and separate provisions of that law restrict federal immigration authorities' access to non-public areas of jails absent a warrant from a federal judge or magistrate.
