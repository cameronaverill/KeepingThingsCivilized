Public safety, in my view, comes from consistent application of the law, not from carving out exceptions tied to someone's immigration status. I worry this approach quietly tells people that some legal obligations can simply be ignored.

Limiting cooperation to what's judicially authorized isn't picking and choosing which laws matter, it's respecting the distinction between civil requests and binding legal process, which I think protects everyone's rights.
