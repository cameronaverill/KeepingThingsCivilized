Could a source be given for the claim in message 4 about what Colorado's sanctuary policy law does or does not restrict regarding jail access for federal agents?

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Colorado's 2019 sanctuary law (HB19-1124, C.R.S. \u00a7\u00a724-76.6-101 to -103) does bar jails from holding someone past their release date on a civil ICE detainer alone, absent a judicial warrant, which runs counter to the claim that the law does not restrict detention past release for immigration purposes. However, independent summaries also indicate the law separately limits ICE access to jail facilities for interviews or other immigration-enforcement purposes unless certain conditions are met, which further complicates the specific framing in the claim about 'access to jails.' Overall, the law appears to impose more restriction on both detention-past-release and facility access than the claim suggests.
