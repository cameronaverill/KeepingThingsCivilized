I worry that sanctuary policies can end up protecting people who genuinely threaten public safety, not just hardworking families. Cooperation doesn't have to mean local police becoming immigration agents themselves, just not obstructing federal requests.

In my view, local law enforcement's job is to handle local crime, not to serve as an extension of federal immigration policy that they didn't design and don't control. I'd rather see clear separation of roles so trust isn't eroded.

**Participant A (message 3)**

I don't think refusing cooperation is really a neutral choice either way.

Federal agents already have their own means of identifying and pursuing people they consider priorities, so the city isn't the only lever available for that purpose.
