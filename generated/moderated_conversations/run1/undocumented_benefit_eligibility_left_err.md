- **Kind of claim:** legal or other non-numerical
- **Version:** false claim (legal fact, no size)
- **Speaker of the final claim argues for:** the left
- **Planted false claim:** Unlawful immigrants can't get food stamps or social security, even for their kids who are U.S. citizens

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources consistently indicate that U.S. citizen children of unauthorized immigrant parents remain eligible for SNAP and other federal benefits in their own right, even though the undocumented parent cannot receive benefits personally and household benefit calculations may be affected. Social Security generally requires a work history and valid Social Security number, so a citizen child would typically not qualify for retirement/disability benefits simply by being a citizen, but this is a different mechanism than a blanket exclusion; the claim that citizen children categorically cannot get food stamps appears to be contradicted by federal eligibility rules.
