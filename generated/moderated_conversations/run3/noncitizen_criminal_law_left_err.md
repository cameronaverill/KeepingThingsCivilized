**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim that noncitizens accused of state or local crimes are not released on bail is likely too broad as stated. Bail practices vary by state, jurisdiction, and the specifics of a case, and noncitizens are in fact released on bail in many such cases.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Evidence does not support a blanket rule that noncitizens accused of state or local crimes are denied bail; eligibility for pretrial release on state charges generally depends on state law, the alleged offense, and flight-risk assessment, and immigration status alone is not a uniform bar. Separately, a federal immigration detainer (an ICE hold request) is distinct from the state bail decision and, by its own terms, is not supposed to determine bail eligibility, though in practice it can result in continued local custody even after state bail is posted in some jurisdictions. Overall, the claim as stated appears too broad.
