**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim in message 4 about Illinois sanctuary law may have it backwards: Illinois's TRUST Act and related provisions generally restrict rather than permit local law enforcement from holding people solely on a civil immigration detainer request. This could be checked against the text of the Illinois TRUST Act.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources, including the Illinois Attorney General's guidance and the text of the Illinois TRUST Act itself, indicate the law generally prohibits, rather than permits, local law enforcement from detaining or continuing to detain someone solely on the basis of a civil immigration detainer. This runs contrary to the claim that the law generally still allows such holds.
