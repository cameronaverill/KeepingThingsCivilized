**AI moderator** (asked for a source)

Could a source be given for the figure of 31,808 declined civil administrative detainers between January 2014 and September 2016?

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

The ICE FY2016 Enforcement and Removal Operations report cites a total of 21,205 declined detainers across 567 counties between January 1, 2014 and September 30, 2016, not 31,808 as stated; the figure given in the claim does not match the official ICE documentation found for this period.
