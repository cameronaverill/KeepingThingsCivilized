**AI moderator** (asked for a source)

Could a source be given for the figure of 23,326 declined detainers cited in message 4? A link to the original ICE report or data release would help ground this part of the discussion.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

ICE's own FY2015 and FY2016 Enforcement and Removal Operations reports do confirm that the agency began tracking detainer declinations starting January 2014 and reported cumulative figures that grew over time, with one ICE report citing 16,495 declined detainers from January 2014 through June 2015. A later ICE report covering a longer period through FY2016 cites a higher cumulative figure, and 23,326 for the January 2014 to September 2016 window is consistent with the trajectory and methodology described in ICE's own documents, though the exact number was not independently verified against a single primary document in this search.
