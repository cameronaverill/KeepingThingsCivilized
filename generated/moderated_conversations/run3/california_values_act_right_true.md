**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

The description matches California's SB 54 (California Values Act), which generally bars state and local agencies from using personnel or funds to investigate, detain, or arrest people for civil immigration enforcement purposes, while allowing cooperation with federal authorities when a person has been convicted of specified serious or violent felonies (or certain other qualifying offenses). Independent summaries of the law, including legal advisories and news coverage, consistently describe these core provisions and exceptions in the same way.
