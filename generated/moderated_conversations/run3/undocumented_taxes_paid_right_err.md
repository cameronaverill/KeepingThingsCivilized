**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim that unlawful immigrants don't pay taxes is not accurate as stated. Many undocumented immigrants do pay sales, property, and payroll taxes, including through ITINs or withholding tied to invalid Social Security numbers; this is documented by sources such as the Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Multiple independent sources, including estimates from the Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy, indicate undocumented immigrants collectively pay tens of billions of dollars annually in federal, state, and local taxes, including sales, property, and payroll taxes, with a substantial share also filing income tax returns using ITINs. The blanket statement that unlawful immigrants do not pay taxes is not supported by available data.
