**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim that undocumented immigrants can typically receive food stamps and Social Security is not accurate as a general statement. Federal law generally makes undocumented immigrants ineligible for SNAP and Social Security benefits for themselves, though mixed-status households may sometimes receive benefits on behalf of a citizen child. This distinction is worth keeping in mind when citing this point.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources consistently indicate undocumented immigrants are not eligible for SNAP (food stamps) or Social Security benefits for themselves under federal law; eligibility is generally restricted to citizens and certain lawfully present immigrants, with Social Security additionally requiring documented work history. A mixed-status household can sometimes receive SNAP on behalf of an eligible U.S. citizen child, but this is narrower than a general claim that unlawful immigrants typically get these benefits, including for their children.
