**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim in message 4 about Illinois law appears to have the mechanism reversed: under Illinois's TRUST Act framework, a judicial warrant is generally what would permit a hold, not prohibit one. It may be worth double-checking the specific statutory language before relying on this point.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources on Illinois's TRUST Act indicate the opposite of what is stated: the law restricts local police from cooperating with federal immigration holds unless a judicial warrant (signed by a judge) has been issued, meaning a judge's warrant is generally what permits detention rather than what blocks it.
