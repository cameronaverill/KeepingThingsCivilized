**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources confirm ICE has published periodic tallies of declined detainers starting in January 2014, with one ICE report citing 16,495 declined detainers from January 2014 through June 2015, and other ICE figures showing varying counts for different date ranges; no independent source matching the specific figure of 21,205 for the January 2014 to September 2016 window was located in available searches. The general pattern of ICE tracking and publicizing declined detainers, including cases involving people with criminal records, is well documented, but the precise number cited could not be independently verified.
