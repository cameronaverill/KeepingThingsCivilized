**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The characterization of Washington's sanctuary-related law in message 4 appears inaccurate: the state's Keep Washington Working Act restricts state and local agencies from sharing personal information for immigration enforcement purposes except in limited circumstances, rather than leaving such sharing unrestricted. This can be checked against the text of that statute.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources confirm Washington's Keep Washington Working Act (RCW 10.93.160) does restrict state and local law enforcement from sharing nonpublic personal information with federal immigration authorities, generally requiring a court order or judicial warrant for such disclosure or for honoring detainer requests, except in limited circumstances like ongoing criminal investigations. This appears to contradict the characterization that the law leaves such data sharing unrestricted and warrant-free.
