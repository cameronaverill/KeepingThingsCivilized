**AI moderator** (said what was wrong)

The claim that local authorities in sanctuary jurisdictions are still required to cooperate with federal agents on such arrests does not match how these policies are generally described; sanctuary jurisdictions typically limit or decline such cooperation, for example by not honoring federal detainer requests. A source describing the specific jurisdiction's policy would help ground this point.

**AI moderator: web-search note** (requested after the reply above)

Independent sources consistently describe sanctuary jurisdictions as policies that limit, restrict, or decline cooperation with federal immigration enforcement, such as refusing to honor ICE detainer requests or hold individuals past release for ICE pickup; there is no general legal requirement that local authorities assist in such arrests. Definitions and practices vary somewhat by jurisdiction, and some sanctuary policies still permit limited cooperation in specific circumstances, but the core framing of these policies is reduced, not mandated, cooperation.
