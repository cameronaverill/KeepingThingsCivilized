I'd rather see police stick to their core job than serve as an extension of a separate federal agency.

If someone here illegally commits a crime, I don't see why local police shouldn't share that information with federal authorities. Public safety has to come first, and I worry these limiting policies send a message that consequences are optional depending on where you live.

I'd rather cities draw a firm boundary now than try to walk it back after trust is already broken.
