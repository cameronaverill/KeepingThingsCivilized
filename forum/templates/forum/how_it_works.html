How this works

What this site is

Two people discuss a proposition in writing. An AI moderator reads each message and may add contributions of its own.

Propositions

Get started by choosing a proposition to discuss or by creating one of your own.

Either participant can end a conversation at any time. That closes it for both of you: it stays readable by the participants, but nobody can post in it. It is kept for the research record.

What the AI moderator does

After each message, the AI moderator looks for problems such as factual errors, unclear statements, and abusive language and decides whether to jump in.

The moderator is not a judge, and it can be wrong. If you think a contribution by the moderator is mistaken, feel free to say so in the conversation.

What is recorded

Your messages and the moderator’s analysis and replies are stored for the research team's possible review.
