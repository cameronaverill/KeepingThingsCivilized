How this works

What this site is

Two people discuss a proposition in writing. An AI moderator reads each message and may add a short note of its own. The site is also part of a research project on whether AI moderators treat different viewpoints equally.

Propositions

Anyone with an account can propose a statement to debate. It appears on the home page straight away; nobody at the site reviews it first, and listing one is not an endorsement. You can create up to {{ max_propositions_per_day }} a day, and each can be up to {{ max_proposition_chars }} characters. If a proposition is abusive, tell the site admin [contact to be added].

Choosing a proposition puts you in a conversation on it. If you already have one open on that proposition, you return to it. If someone else is already waiting to discuss it, you are paired with them. Otherwise you wait, and the conversation starts when someone else picks the same proposition. A waiting conversation does not expire.

You do not have to argue for or against anything, and nobody is told which side you take. Each of you is shown only as “You” and “The other participant”. Only the two people in a conversation can read it.

Either of you can end a conversation at any time. That closes it for both of you: it stays readable, but nobody can post in it, and it is kept for the research record.

What the AI moderator does

After each message it looks for problems such as a factual error, abusive language, flooding the conversation, or an unanswered question, and decides whether to say anything. It can only reply to a person’s message, never to its own, and it posts at most once per message.

It is not a judge and it can be wrong. It is instructed to treat both sides of an argument the same way and never to take a side. If you think a note is mistaken, say so in the conversation.

Limits

  • Each message can be up to {{ max_message_chars|floatformat:"0g" }} characters (about 500 words). Longer messages are not sent; the page tells you by how much to shorten yours, and keeps your text.
  • You can post one message every {{ min_seconds }} seconds, counted from your own previous message.
  • A conversation closes after {{ max_messages }} messages and has two participants.
  • Nothing stops you posting several messages in a row.

These limits keep the discussion readable and keep the AI moderator’s running costs low. Whenever you cannot post, the page tells you why and what to do next.

Cost and pauses

The moderator runs on a small daily budget. If it is used up, moderation pauses and the page says so. Your messages are still posted.

What is recorded

Your messages, the moderator’s analysis and its replies are stored so the research team can study how moderation treats different viewpoints. [Retention period and who can see the data: to be written before launch.] Your email address is used only for account confirmation and password reset.
